How borrowing works

Four steps from search to shelf.

  1. Join the library

    Fill in the registration form. Your membership card is ready at the desk the same day.

  2. Find your book

    Search by title, author or ISBN. Note the call number shown on the book.

  3. Borrow or reserve

    Pick up available copies at the desk. If all copies are issued, reserve one and we will message you.

  4. Return or renew

    Return the book by the due date, or renew it online if nobody is waiting.

Library rules and hours

Opening hours

Monday to Friday, 9:00 am to 7:00 pm. Saturday, 9:00 am to 2:00 pm. Closed on Sundays and public holidays.

Late returns

A fine of Rs. 2 per day applies after the due date. Fines above Rs. 100 pause your borrowing until they are paid.

Lost or damaged books

Tell the desk as soon as it happens. You pay the current price of the book, or replace it with the same edition.

Reference books

Encyclopaedias, journals and rare books are for reading inside the library only.
