Library Policies

Rules & Operating Hours

Clear, accessible guidelines for circulation, renewal, and library conduct.

Operating Hours & Reading Room Schedule ▼

Monday through Friday: 9:00 AM – 7:00 PM. Saturday: 9:00 AM – 2:00 PM. Closed on Sundays and official statutory government holidays.

Overdue Policies & Fine Calculation ▼

An overdue charge of Rs. 2.00 per day is automatically computed for each volume kept past the due date. Accounts with accumulated fines exceeding Rs. 100 will temporarily pause new checkout privileges.

Care of Volumes & Replacement Terms ▼

Borrowers are responsible for returning items in clean condition. If a volume is damaged or lost, the borrower may provide an identical edition or pay the publisher replacement value.

Non-Circulating Reference Collections ▼

Encyclopaedias, historical manuscripts, current issues of print journals, and competitive exam question banks are strictly reserved for in-library reading room consultation.
